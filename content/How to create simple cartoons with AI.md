
Someone recently asked me how they can create simple cartoons with AI

this is the easiest/best solution I can see that's free

  

Flux AI Image generator(https://fluxaiimagegenerator.com/flux-schnell) is free for up to 5 images a day, and ChatGPT(free) is good enough to write prompts for it. While it does have image generation, it is not nearly as good as Flux.(though it still does a good job)

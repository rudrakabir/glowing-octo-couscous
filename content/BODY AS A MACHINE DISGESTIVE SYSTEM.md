 **Body as a machine.

  

A long tube in my body, containing the outside in my insides. 

  

A machine with a clear purpose - to sort and process foreign objects and extract maximal value from them, leaving behind only toxic waste, all while keeping it safely contained to minimise harm to the inside of my body.

But the hole of the donut, is the tube that connects the mouth that ends in the anus. One long uninterrupted hole where food goes in, and shit comes out. Topologically, the digestive system, while “inside” our body

The stomach - a digester tank with a slurry mixer (crazy how the word for digester itself comees from the processes of the stomach, with tightly titrated chemicals that would corrode the inside of my body without a regenerative lining that renews continuously
